# 📊 Power BI Analytics Portfolio

### Interactive Dashboards | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence

Welcome to my **Power BI Analytics Portfolio**, a collection of three interactive dashboards developed to explore datasets, track key performance indicators (KPIs), and present business insights through data visualization.

This repository showcases my hands-on practice with **Microsoft Power BI**, data cleaning and transformation using **Power Query**, and basic calculations using **DAX**.

The dashboards cover three different analytical domains:

- 👥 **HR Analytics** — workforce metrics and employee attrition
- ☕ **Coffee Shop Revenue Analytics** — revenue patterns and order performance
- 🛒 **E-Commerce Sales Analytics** — sales, profit, and product performance

My goal is to transform raw data into clear, interactive reports that make patterns, comparisons, and trends easier to understand.

## 1. HR Analytics Dashboard

### Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard presents workforce KPIs and employee attrition patterns in an interactive format.

It allows users to examine attrition across different demographic and job-related categories and explore how workforce metrics vary between groups.

### Key Metrics

- Employee count
- Total attrition
- Attrition rate
- Average employee age
- Average salary
- Average years at the company

### Analysis Performed

The dashboard explores attrition across:

- Education
- Age groups
- Salary ranges
- Gender
- Years at the company
- Job roles

### Interactive Features

- KPI cards for a quick overview of workforce metrics
- Department selection to explore relevant employee groups
- Visual breakdowns of attrition across multiple categories
- Interactive charts for comparing workforce patterns

### Business Questions Explored

- How does attrition vary across age groups?
- Which job roles show different attrition patterns?
- How does attrition vary across salary ranges?
- How does attrition differ across education and gender categories?
- How do years at the company relate to observed attrition patterns?

### Value of the Dashboard

The dashboard organizes several workforce indicators in one place, making it easier to explore attrition patterns and compare employee groups.

**Important:** These visualizations show associations in the available data. They do not, by themselves, establish why employees leave or prove that a particular factor causes attrition.

## 🔄 Analytical Workflow

The general workflow for these dashboard projects can be represented as:

**Dataset → Data Preparation → Data Modeling and Measures → Visualization → Interactive Analysis**

1. **Understand the dataset:** Identify the available fields, categories, dates, and business metrics.
2. **Prepare the data:** Review and clean the source data, applying Power Query transformations where used.
3. **Build the report:** Organize relevant metrics, charts, and KPI cards into a dashboard layout.
4. **Create calculations:** Use basic DAX measures where applicable to calculate or summarize KPIs.
5. **Add interactivity:** Configure relevant filters and slicers to support exploration.
6. **Review the output:** Check that displayed values, categories, and filters behave as expected.

This describes the overall analytical workflow; the exact transformations and calculations may differ between dashboards.
